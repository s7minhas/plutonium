# Author Biographies

**Ha Eun Choi** is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration at the University of Potsdam, Germany. Her research lies at the intersection of international political economy, international organizations, and foreign policy, with particular interest in how international organizations and states address human rights and gender equality through institutional design and economic and diplomatic instruments.

*Photo: choi.jpg*

---

**Scott de Marchi** is Professor of Political Science and Director of the Decision Science program at Duke University. His research focuses on mathematical methods, including bargaining theory, computational social science, game theory, and machine learning. Substantively, he examines decision-making in contexts ranging from the American Congress to crisis bargaining and interstate conflict.

*Photo: demarchi.jpg*

---

**Max Gallop** is a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in the Department of Politics and International Relations at the University of Strathclyde. His research focuses on explaining and predicting civil and interstate conflict, particularly by applying bargaining theory and network models.

*Photo: gallop.jpg*

---

**Shahryar Minhas** is an Associate Professor of International Relations in the Department of Political Science and the Social Science Data Analytics Program at Michigan State University. His research sits at the intersection of political methodology and international relations, with a focus on network analysis and statistical modeling of conflict, cooperation, and international institutions.
